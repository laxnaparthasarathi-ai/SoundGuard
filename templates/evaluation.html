About the Model

SoundGuard currently uses a Decision Tree machine-learning classifier trained on numerical audio features.

The dataset contains five ESC-50 sound categories: dog, engine, glass breaking, rain, and siren.

This is a baseline machine-learning implementation, not a deep-learning model. Further evaluation on a larger, independent dataset is required before real-world use.
